  "Motivation": [
    "Conventional toasters accumulate bread crumbs at the bottom of the toasting chamber, which creates a fire hazard under high temperature baking conditions.",
    "Cleaning existing toasters is inconvenient, often requiring the entire toaster to be turned upside down and shaken to remove crumbs.",
    "Even frequent cleaning does not ensure removal of all crumbs, leading to potential pest infestation (cockroaches or mice), contamination, and health risks.",
    "Conventional toasters require manual removal of toasted bread, which is inconvenient and unsafe when the toaster is hot."

  "Novelty": [
    "A toaster design that automatically discharges toasted bread through a door at the outlet of the toasting chamber.",
    "Integration of a door opening structure that combines a door locking mechanism, a lift door opening mechanism, a magnetic suction mechanism, a power switch mechanism, and a PCB control board.",
    "Use of a magnetic suction mechanism controlled by the PCB to coordinate timing of door release and bread discharge.",
    "A bread tray positioned at the front side of the toaster to directly collect bread and crumbs, preventing accumulation inside the toasting chamber."
  ],
  
  "Key Invention Features": [
    "A housing with a toasting chamber having an upper inlet for feeding sliced bread and a lower outlet for discharging sliced bread.",
    "A door at the outlet for opening and closing the toasting chamber.",
    "A door opening structure comprising: a door locking mechanism, a lift door opening mechanism, a magnetic suction mechanism, a power switch mechanism, and a PCB control board.",
    "The lift door opening mechanism is slidably mounted outside the toasting chamber.",
    "The magnetic suction mechanism and the power switch mechanism are positioned below the lift door opening mechanism.",
    "The PCB control board is electrically connected with the magnetic suction mechanism.",
    "When the lift door opening mechanism is pressed down, the door locking mechanism disengages from a locking shaft, the power switch mechanism activates, the magnetic suction mechanism engages the lift mechanism until toasting is complete, and then the PCB de-energizes the magnetic suction mechanism to allow the lift mechanism to rise and open the door for bread discharge."
