  "Motivation": [
    "Conventional grinder-dosers rely on multiple screw-fastened assemblies between the upper grinder holder, the head, and the grinder itself, which increases complexity.",
    "The need for frequent cleaning of the grinding chamber introduces repeated disassembly and reassembly, creating opportunities for misalignment.",
    "Misalignment of grinders during reassembly leads to irregular particle size distribution of ground coffee, resulting in inconsistent taste and dosing.",
    "Prior art requires precise machining and assembly tolerances to maintain grinder parallelism, which increases manufacturing cost and still risks inconsistency."

    "A redesigned grinder-doser head that provides a single parallel coupling point between the upper grinder and the head, reducing assembly errors.",
    "The upper grinder is formed by a toothed annular portion housed within a hollow annular portion, itself surrounded by an annular portion that rests directly on a horizontal annular seat.",
    "The flat surface of the upper grinder’s annular portion is rectified and collimated with the plane passing through the ends of the grinder teeth, ensuring precise alignment.",
    "This structural arrangement simplifies removal and reinstallation of the upper grinder while maintaining consistent parallelism and grind uniformity."
  ],
  
  "Key Invention Features": [
    "A body housing an electric motor with a shaft integral in rotation with a lower grinder holder.",
    "A lower grinder joined to the lower grinder holder.",
    "A head containing the lower grinder holder and lower grinder, the head having a horizontal annular seat at its lateral edge.",
    "An upper grinder formed by: (i) a toothed annular portion, (ii) a hollow annular portion housing the toothed portion, and (iii) an outer annular portion resting on the annular seat.",
    "The flat surface of the outer annular portion of the upper grinder is rectified and collimated with a plane passing through the ends of the teeth of the toothed annular portion."
