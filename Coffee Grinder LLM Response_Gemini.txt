    "US 11,647,866 B2",
    "GRINDER/DOSER DEVICE FOR COFFEE BEANS"
  ],
  "Motivation": [
    "Prior coffee grinder-dosers require perfect execution and assembly of parts to ensure parallelism between the grinders, which is difficult to achieve.",
    "The existing system for fastening and removing the upper grinder from the grinding chamber leads to a 'double possibility of error' during assembly and maintenance.",
    "These errors can result in an irregular particle size of the ground coffee, leading to inconsistent taste, irregular doses, and powder residues in the cup."
  ],
  "Novelty": [
    "A grinder-doser that achieves a regular particle size.",
    "An apparatus that simplifies the removal and coupling of the upper grinder.",
    "A design that uses a single parallel coupling point between the upper grinder and the head to guarantee parallelism."
  ],
  "Key Invention Features": [
    "A body which internally houses an electric motor having a shaft which is integral in rotation with a lower grinder holder to which the lower grinder is joined.",
    "A lower grinder holder, and a lower grinder being housed inside a head which has a horizontal annular seat in a lateral edge.",
    "An upper grinder housed in the horizontal annular seat.",
    "The upper grinder is formed by a toothed annular portion housed inside a hollow annular portion.",
    "The hollow annular portion is surrounded by an annular portion which rests on the annular seat.",
    "A flat surface of the annular portion of the upper grinder is rectified and collimated with a plane passing through the ends of the teeth of the toothed annular portion."
  ]
